# Mythic encounters · v0.4

An original connected river journey, not a chronological retelling of scripture. Naga and vanara conflicts concern specific groups; they are not inherently evil or automatically allied to asuras. All encounters remain open for prototype testing.

## Encounter rules

| Encounter | Gameplay |
|---|---|
| Hungry vanara band | Dedicated raider waves; a raider reaching the temple steals 20 offerings as well as damaging shield. Story resolves through food and safe boundaries. |
| Interrupted rite | Ritual-breaking rakshasas threaten the sanctuary for three shield each. |
| Disputed naga pools | Guardians can emerge 32% along the route. Mist bearers conceal nearby allies; Saraswati reveals them. |
| Return the river stone | Completing the escort makes naga guardians withdraw from remaining waves. Asura opponents remain. |
| Mayadhara / Nishachara / Rudhiraksha | Former commanders now enter mid-wave as elites in the second encounter of their regions. Their original mirror, night and changing-seal mechanics remain. |
| Mahishasura | Changes buffalo/warrior appearance and movement. Durga’s empowered strike permanently breaks his damage-blocking boon. Final wave requires Durga and grants a full meter. |
| Raktabija | Damage produces at most one echo per three seconds, capped at six living echoes. Temporary Call Kali action suppresses new echoes for ten seconds; 18-second cooldown. |
| Vritra | Starts with two coils; gains one every six seconds, capped at five. Each reduces incoming damage by 13%. Indra’s actual hits remove coils, at most once per second. |

Shrine damage, timings, invulnerability shields and non-graphic magical seeds are invented game adaptations. Mahishasura’s special counter is not a claim that other goddesses are incapable of harming him in religious tradition. Kali is a story-specific ally, not an eighth buildable shrine.

## Foundations consulted

- [Mahishasura and Durga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahishasura): shape-changing buffalo adversary and Durga’s confrontation.
- [Raktabija](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raktab%C4%ABja): multiplying blood and Kali’s role in preventing replication; adapted as crimson magic seeds without gore.
- [Vritra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vritra): obstruction of the waters and Indra’s victory.

Iconography and cultural framing should receive human review before a commercial release. These sources establish the broad narrative associations, not every game rule.

## Other changes

- Varuna: low damage, Wet for five seconds, one backward wash per foe per four seconds across all Varuna shrines; flying foes resist, bosses move less. Wet still strengthens Indra and does not extinguish Agni.
- Targeting pauses time; area interventions show the affected radius and require confirmation. Arrow keys can select path bends; Escape cancels.
- Repeated Durga empowerment no longer spends a full meter for nothing.
- Escorts can only dispatch from wave two until two waves before the end. Final-clear completion requires at least 75% of the crossing for its reward.
- Difficulty-specific victory records supplement existing saved restoration. Active v3 battles restart; completed temples remain.

## Validation

Automated engine checks cover all 15 Normal encounters with finite offerings, plus myth counters, bounded replication, emergence, reconciliation, washback resistance, save/resume and existing mechanics. This is not browser playtesting or a claim about human enjoyment or phone readability.
