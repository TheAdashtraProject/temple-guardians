# Playability pass · v0.5

- Persistent Continue / Replay / Results controls directly below the battlefield. Inspecting the restored scene no longer hides the route back to results.
- Durga aiming checks battle state, shrine recharge and eligible visible targets. Invalid targeting clears automatically. Cancel, Escape and the main pause control offer a route back to play.
- The bell names its reveal and charge effects on the control, produces expanding ripples, and has a distinct sound when audio is enabled.
- Mira introduces herself once. Contextual briefings and occasional non-blocking field lines introduce Kapi (male vanara scout), Dharan (male yaksha guardian) and Leela (female mountain keeper). Ordinary waves have no new dialogue modal.
- Victories refer back to the vanara settlement, return of the river stone, Dharan joining, the town reopening and the final climb. Pilgrims visibly return to the restored crossing; Dharan appears beside later battlefields after his chapter is completed.
- Approaches now last four waves, escort encounters six, and commander battles eight. The vanara approach has faster arrivals; the first town encounter splits enemies between two streets that join at the sanctuary.
- Mahishasura telegraphs a two-second charge, then recovers. Durga interrupts a charge or deals 60% more damage during recovery. His escorts arrive while he advances. The final-wave Durga requirement applies only to his actual commander encounter.
- Varuna’s washback interpolates visibly, Indra’s water synergy shows a short cue, Saraswati’s supported shrines show the speed bonus, Vayu’s impact is stronger visually, and enemy hits and movement have lightweight feedback. Existing illustrated assets are retained; this is not a full frame-by-frame animation replacement.
- Unfinished v4 battles restart for the new wave structure. Restored temples, lamps and companion introduction state persist.

Validation: 33 automated checks, including all 15 Normal encounters, target availability edge cases, contextual cast coverage, street convergence, charge interruption and recovery damage. No browser playthrough was performed for this release.
